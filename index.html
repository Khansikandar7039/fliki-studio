✨ Fliki Studio

Free personal text-to-video studio · Runs in your browser

No accountNo creditsLocal processing

1. Script & scenes

Paste your script. Each non-empty line becomes one editable scene. Hindi and English text are supported.

2. Voice & audio

Browser voice preview uses voices installed on your phone. For voice inside the exported video, upload a narration audio file. Built-in speech preview cannot reliably be recorded into the video by mobile browsers.

3. Images & scene visuals

Upload a separate image for each scene below. You can also use the suggested image prompt to create an image in any image tool and upload it here. This offline app does not generate AI images itself.

4. Preview & editor

Preview uses your scene text and uploaded image. Export keeps this format and burns captions into the video.

5. Export video

MP4 is used only if your browser supports MP4 recording. Otherwise this app exports WebM. Keep this page open and your screen awake during export.

Ready. Build scenes, add images/audio, then export.

What this free version can and cannot do

Included: editable scenes, image uploads, Hindi/English browser voice preview, uploaded narration/music, burned-in subtitles, landscape/portrait/square canvas, preview and browser video export.

Not included: cloud AI image generation, premium neural voices, voice cloning, AI avatars or guaranteed MP4 on every phone. Those require external services, models or more processing than a static free website can provide.
